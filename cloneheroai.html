AI-assisted music analysis

CloneHeroAI · Guitar Intelligence

Any song.
Your chart.

The goal: A machine understands musical rhythm and harmonics, and translates that knowledge into playable charts for the user.

Read development notes
PROJECT STATUS: IN DEVELOPMENT. CloneHeroAI is an active engineering project and is not yet represented as a finished or reliable release.

From audio to playable chart

The project is moving away from trying to guess chart patterns from a full song mix. The pipeline is being organized around identifying guitar-focused audio and learning useful performance details before chart composition.

Project capabilities and goals

AreaObjective
Audio analysisExtract useful musical information from songs, including event timing and rhythm.
Guitar-focused inputSeparate the guitar from drums, bass, vocals, and other instruments before analysis where possible.
Guitar intelligenceLearn information about pitch, onsets, playing techniques, and overlapping notes from aligned training examples.
Chart compositionConvert predicted events into five-fret note patterns that are playable and musically coherent.
Player-focused toolingWork toward options such as regenerating rough sections and supporting different charting styles as quality allows.

Research direction

The core challenge is not merely creating notes, but producing notes that correspond to the performance. Earlier chart-generation experiments made the gap clear: a pipeline needs useful guitar information, timing, and event structure before it can make strong charting decisions.

Current engineering priority: the AI is not smart yet. More training and further analysis on model actions, reasoning, and database are to be completed in the future.
