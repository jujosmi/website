Company overview

About Jujosmi Labs

Jujosmi Labs is an independent software engineering and development initiative focused on AI-assisted software, practical tools, and experimental technology.

Purpose

The lab exists to turn ideas into testable software, measure what the software actually does, and improve it based on results. Projects may begin as experiments and move toward practical releases as their behavior, usability, and reliability mature.

Engineering principles

Build with purpose

Define what a project should do and make its scope visible.

Measure the outcome

Use tests, baselines, diagnostics, and real examples to understand performance.

Improve honestly

Record limitations and failures instead of treating experiments as finished products.

Current work

CloneHeroAI is an active project focused on guitar intelligence and chart generation. Trading AI is a research initiative for market analysis and strategy evaluation. Their status and scope are listed in the project directory.

Jujosmi Labs was founded in 2026 as a home for independent software and AI development.
